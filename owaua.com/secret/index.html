what it actually does

Ping it when you want it in the conversation. It answers in the voice and language that server already chose.

  • it waits until you askPing it in a server and it replies. A lonely @owaua with nothing else gets a tiny local “yeah?” — no model call. Other bots are ignored. DMs only work if the operator turns on BOT_ALLOW_DMS=1.
  • it remembers your thread, and a little of the roomYour history with it is still just you, in that channel: up to 20 turns, they fade after a week, and the file tops out at 10,000 records. It also keeps a short recent line from that channel so a ping is not blind. The person next to you still has their own thread and their own persona.
  • a few voices, and a few brainsPick rudeish low, rudeish medium, rudeish high, nerdish, flirty, irritating, cute, or normal for yourself. The person-in-the-room voice is off by default; !human on adds it. Full mode is a separate opt-in for approved users.
  • it can answer in your languageAdmins set a full language name, like !language hungarian. When matching art exists, owaua can change that server’s bot avatar and banner too, so it looks like it belongs there.
  • a song in voice, if you ask!music plays a single YouTube video or Twitter/X post with video, or a supported audio attachment. Pause, resume, restart, skip, stop, now, and leave. No name searches or playlists.
  • short answers, on purposeNormal hangout replies stay short and finish the sentence. Gemini hangout can look at one image. Approved people can open !full mode on in the designated channels. Full mode shares the same rate limits.

how you talk to it

Each command has its own 25-second per-user cooldown. Changing server settings needs Manage Server. Music controls only work if you are in the same voice channel as the bot.

!help

anyone The list, when you forget.

!persona rudeish low|medium|high

you Hear or change your general-audience voice. Also nerdish, flirty, irritating, cute, and normal.

!human on|off

you Turn on the person-in-the-room voice, or go back to the usual hangout-bot voice. It is off by default.

!language <full name>|reset

manage server Set or clear the server’s reply language, and the matching profile art when it exists.

!music <YouTube/X URL or attachment>

in voice Put one supported track on. Or pause, resume, restart, skip, stop, now, and leave.

!memory erase

manage server Wipe this server’s stored chats and recent channel lines.

!memory erase mine

anyone Wipe your own thread, including DMs when those are on.

!reset all

manage server Wipes this server’s stored chats, channel lines, reply language, and profile art, and stops its music.

!security status|pause|resume

owner How busy it is, and an emergency pause if something is going wrong.

!shutdown

owner Stop the bot.

!pricing

owner Show current model pricing.

!owner's note

anyone A short note from the person who made it.

the limits, said plainly

These exist so one person cannot melt it, and so it can stay cheap enough to keep around. They are request ceilings, not a promise of free usage.

  • eight AI tries a minute, per userThat counter lives in the running process and resets when it restarts. There is no separate persisted daily or ten-minute quota. Full mode shares the same limits.
  • three AI users at onceAt most three users can occupy an AI reply slot at a time. A command has a separate 25-second per-user cooldown.
  • music stays smallSupported YouTube or Twitter/X video links must resolve to a single track under 15 minutes. Audio attachments and downloaded audio are capped at 20 MiB, with at most two ordinary music sessions. Native playback needs Linux and FFmpeg.

It can be wrong with a straight face. Check anything that matters.

what it keeps

It holds your thread with it, and a short log of recent lines in that channel, so the next reply is not from nowhere. Your persona stays yours. That lives in a local SQLite file. Old turns fall off on their own. !memory erase clears a server’s stored conversations and those lines. !memory erase mine clears yours.

Hangout requests go to the configured model with provider-side storage turned off. Music requests land in a small audit log for the operator. Passwords, signed media links, and provider error text are not written down.

things people actually ask

Does it reply to every message?

No. Mention it when you want a reply. It does keep a short recent line from the channel so it is not blind when you do. DMs only if the operator has turned those on. Other bots never get an answer.

Why didn’t it answer?

Make sure you actually pinged it. Give it a moment — it shows typing while it works. Check the rate limits and command cooldowns. Other bots are ignored on purpose.

Can I change how it sounds?

Yes. !persona rudeish low, medium, or high, or nerdish, flirty, irritating, cute, or normal. The person-in-the-room voice is off by default; !human on brings it back.

Does the music actually play?

Yes. It joins your voice channel and streams the track. No playlists, no queue of the next ten songs. You need to be in voice, and it needs Connect and Speak there.

Can I trust every reply?

No. It can be sure and still wrong. If something matters, check it yourself, or ask a person.

Where do I say something is broken?

The Discord server, or the person who made it: Discord ckazros, ckazros@owaua.com. Say what you tried and what happened. Please don’t paste tokens or private chats into a public channel.

or run it yourself

owaua is open source, MIT. You will want Python 3.11+, Discord’s Message Content Intent, the provider keys, and Linux plus FFmpeg if you want music. After that, it is just talking.
