the agreement

Last updated September 23, 2026. If you invite owaua, talk to it, or keep it in a server, you agree to this.

01

what this covers

This is the agreement for the official owaua Discord bot, the app you invite from this site, client id 1442127404607737999. The website and the owaua Discord server sit with the same person.

Discord has its own terms, and you have to follow those too. If you run a copy of the code yourself, you are the operator of that copy. These terms are for the official bot.

02

who can use it

You need a Discord account, and you need to be old enough for Discord. That means 13 or older. Please don’t use the bot if you are under 13.

If you add it to a server, you are saying you have permission to do that, and that people in the server may be talking to a bot that stores recent chat.

03

what the bot is

owaua is a hangout companion. You ping it in a server, and DMs work only when the operator has turned them on. It can switch between rudeish low / medium / high, nerdish, flirty, irritating, cute, and normal voices, answer in a chosen server language, play bounded YouTube or Twitter/X video and supported audio attachments in a voice channel, and remember up to 20 records per channel + user plus a short log of recent channel lines. Normal hangout replies are short. Gemini hangout can look at one image. Approved users can opt into bounded full mode in designated channels. Commands start with !. There are no slash commands.

It isn’t a person, and it isn’t a moderator. Don’t treat what it says as advice. It can be wrong. It’s told to hang out, not to help.

04

talking to it

Messages you send to the bot, usually pings, and DMs only when those are on, can be stored locally. In a server channel, a short recent line can be stored too, so a later ping has some context. Up to 12 records from your thread, and up to 12 of those channel lines, can go back to the model, with a seven-day expiry. !memory erase mine removes your own history and your channel lines.

Please don’t send passwords, tokens, private keys, or anything you would not want saved. Someone with Manage Server can run !memory erase for that server. DMs are not wiped by that command.

There are request ceilings: eight AI-admission attempts per user per minute in the running process, with no separate persisted daily or ten-minute quota. There can be three AI users in flight at once, and each command has a 25-second per-user cooldown. Hitting a limit is not a ban. Trying to break it, using the bot to spam a channel, or burning API credits on junk is.

More on storage is in the privacy policy.

05

please don’t burn the API

owaua is a hangout bot. Every reply costs real money. You do not get a free model, a homework mill, a benchmark harness, or a toy for wasting tokens.

This part is strict. Using it for junk that burns API credits — instead of hanging out — is abuse. No debate. “I was just testing” does not count.

That includes looping it, farming it, pinging it for nothing, dumping huge pastes, encode-and-decode junk, jailbreak marathons, making it generate walls of filler, running scripts or extra accounts at it, and turning on expensive modes then spamming search, tools, or long thinking on garbage.

We can ignore you, wipe memory, pull the bot from the server, and stop answering you with no warning. The rate limit is not a free pass to keep doing it.

06

if you run a server

If you own or moderate a server with owaua in it, you are responsible for how it is used there. That includes !language, !persona, !music, and !memory erase.

There is no nuke, kick, ban, or other moderation command. Language is server-wide, not per user. All personas are general-audience voices. The person-in-the-room voice is off unless a user turns it on with !human on.

Please don’t use the bot to harass people, raid, spam, or dodge Discord’s rules.

07

please don’t

Don’t use owaua to:

break the rules

The law, Discord’s terms, or these ones.

burn the API

No looping, farming, junk prompts, scripts, extra accounts, or using it as a free ChatGPT. Hang out, or don’t use it. We will cut you off, with no warning.

unsafe content

Do not use the bot to request or distribute illegal, abusive, or otherwise unsafe content.

attack the service

No scraping, flooding, or trying to knock the host or other users over.

impersonate

Don’t pretend to be owaua, the operator, or someone else.

dump other people’s data

Don’t feed it private stuff they did not choose to share with a bot.

steal media

Don’t ask it to fetch or share copyrighted audio or files you do not have the right to use.

ask for real harm

Malware, exploits, or instructions for hurting people or breaking systems in the real world.

We can ignore messages, reset memory, pull the bot from a server, or stop answering you.

08

what it says

Replies are generated. They can be biased, outdated, or just false. Please don’t treat them as medical, legal, financial, or crisis help. If you need a human, talk to a human.

If a first-person self-harm message looks like current risk and not a joke, the bot may send emergency-services text instead of a generated reply. That is not a substitute for calling local emergency services.

You are responsible for what you do with an answer. If you paste it somewhere, that is on you.

09

the official bot vs running it yourself

If you self-host, you are responsible for your tokens, your SQLite file, your users, and your own terms. The official bot is the one invited from this site.

We can change features, limits, models, and availability. We can shut the official bot off.

owaua runs on Discord and talks to model providers. Music uses yt-dlp against YouTube. Their terms apply to what passes through them. We are not Discord, and we are not those vendors.

Partnerships listed on this site are separate projects. They have their own services and policies.

To the extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for what the bot says, stores, deletes, or fails to do — including lost messages, bad advice, or a command that did something you did not want.

If a place you live does not allow some of that, those parts do not apply there. The rest still does.
